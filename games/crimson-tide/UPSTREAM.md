# Crimson Tide — OpenArcade edition

Original MIT-licensed real-time strategy game by AscendRyan. Pinned source: https://github.com/AscendRyan/opensource-rts-game/tree/f012ede31602b54fa535e95340065065c54553d6. See LICENSE for the complete copyright and permission notice. Includes the six original campaign missions, three factions, AI skirmishes, economy, terrain, and procedural renderer. Uses original upstream SVG logo and code-generated graphics, no third-party dependencies or external runtime requests. No commercial Command & Conquer/StarCraft artwork or audio bundled. Original upstream faction and unit naming retained; this is not affiliated with those games.

OpenArcade adaptations: responsive HUD, native pointer selection and command mode, touch pan controls, gamepad camera and center-reticle controls, battle checkpoint with reference-preserving data-only serialization, isolated settings and campaign storage, offline worker. Battle saves preserve map, ore, fog, unit/building identity, queues, projectiles, AI state and camera; randomness after restoring is not deterministic. One rolling local checkpoint per browser, manually save or autosave every 30 seconds; completed matches clear it. No cloud or external services. Controller covers battle actions, not complete menu/build-card navigation. No audio (upstream has none). Physical mobile and full campaign verification still needed.
