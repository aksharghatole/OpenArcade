Make your move.

Tap a marked square, or use arrow keys and Enter/Space. Gamepad: D-pad or left stick to move, A to place, B to undo. Match saves automatically on this device.

Surround opposing discs with your own to flip them. A player with no legal move passes. Most discs wins when neither player can move.
