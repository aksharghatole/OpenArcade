Join the numbers and get to the 2048 tile!

How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one!

OpenArcade edition: An independent, locally hosted adaptation of the original MIT-licensed 2048. Use arrow keys, WASD, swipe, direction buttons or a standard controller. Your current board and best score are saved on this device. MIT license · Pinned upstream source.

Created by Gabriele Cirulli. Based on 1024 by Veewo Studio and conceptually similar to Threes by Asher Vollmer.
